## What is this?

## Why use TCPL?
* **Zero Attribution:** Unlike MIT or Apache, you don't have to keep the author's name. You can pretend you wrote the whole thing yourself.
* **Pure Freedom:** Use it for your multi-billion dollar corporation or your world-domination plot. 
* **Legal Ghosting:** The author has explicitly abandoned all interest. Do not email them. They will not help you.
